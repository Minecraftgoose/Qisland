Qisland — third-party attribution / 第三方署名与许可
================================================================

本目录（LICENSES/）是仓库里所有第三方许可全文的集中存放处。
CI 在组装 NSIS 打包目录时会把本目录全部文件复制进
「软件/Qisland/LICENSES/」，由 NSIS 释放到安装目录的 LICENSES\，
因此安装包里同样带有全部许可全文（OPPO Sans 许可要求任何副本
都保留版权声明与协议全文）。安装包的 assets\ 只放程序运行时读取的
两个字体与通知音效，不混入许可文本或图标原图。

----------------------------------------------------------------
Both fonts are loaded privately at runtime with AddFontResourceExW
(FR_PRIVATE) and removed on shutdown. Neither is installed system-wide.
Keep assets/ next to Qisland.exe; missing fonts fall back gracefully
(OPPO Sans -> Segoe UI / Microsoft YaHei, FA -> geometric icons).
The notification sound is optional in the same way: if assets/notice.wav is
absent, notifications stay silent instead of failing.
