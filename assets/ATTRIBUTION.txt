----------------------------------------------------------------
Both fonts are loaded privately at runtime with AddFontResourceExW
(FR_PRIVATE) and removed on shutdown. Neither is installed system-wide.
Keep assets/ next to Qisland.exe; missing fonts fall back gracefully
(OPPO Sans -> Segoe UI / Microsoft YaHei, FA -> geometric icons).
The notification sound is optional in the same way: if assets/notice.mp3 is
absent, notifications stay silent instead of failing.
